## Decisions made by this plan

The executor applies these; the PR body lists them so the owner can overturn any.

1. **Nine pages.** Staff: `work/capture.md` (sending captures, printing sheets, My captures and My printer: what every capturing person needs; **My printer** shows only to people who hold **Uploads documents**), `work/capture-inbox.md` (the **To process** inbox and a capture's page: the daily work of whoever processes a unit's captures), `work/capture-validate.md` (the **To validate** list, the data read from forms and the mark on the document: a separate job with its own decisions). Administrators: one page per Administration entry (**Capture channels**, **Separator sheets**, **Capture templates**, **Capture flows**), because each is its own screen with its own settings. Plus `quickstart-capture.md` (a channel and a flow, a stack scanned, split, read, checked, filed) and `examples/incoming-mail.md` (registration labels, separator sheets, a form read with a template, a flow that files by rules). Registry was 4 pages for 4 screens; Capture has 9 screens and two walk-throughs.
2. **Sidebar place.** Staff pages after `dms/work/correspondents`; administrator pages after `dms/admin/registers`; the quickstart after `dms/quickstart-admin`; the example after `dms/examples/loan-file-with-keel`. No new sidebar group: the Registry pages set the pattern.
3. **The demo.** The existing local demo city hall (its database already holds the people, units, nomenclator, folders, document types and the Registry) is upgraded in place. The worktree `$DOCSWT` is moved to `6ecc28c7` (detached), not replaced: it holds the venv, the built frontend and the running demo. The DMS main checkout `/home/administrator/work/flavius/surogate-dms` is never touched. Production is never used and gets no writes.
4. **What the demo really runs.** Barcodes (zxing-cpp) and OCR (PaddleOCR with the two pinned PP-OCRv6 models) run for real. Handwriting is read by a local stand-in, `fake_vision_server.py`, which answers the text the demo forms were written with and never calls out; the production model's address and key are never used, read or stored (never source anything in `~/.config/surogate-dms/`). There is no Keel connection: the Keel step is pictured with the warning the check really gives, and the guide describes what it does when Keel is connected. The mailbox channel is saved switched off and is never read.
5. **A new document type for the demo, Application,** with no default folder. Every older demo type has a default folder, and a type's default folder wins over a flow's destination, so without it the pictures could not show a rule sending a part to the year's dosar.
6. **Printer addresses** show the reserved host `dms.example.org`: the demo API runs with `DMS_PUBLIC_URL=https://dms.example.org` (the browser still talks to `127.0.0.1:18080`).
7. **Romanian on screen.** Allowed: the word `captura` in the Linux command of the **My printer** picture (`lpadmin -p captura ...`, a printer name the English UI prints), the five terms of the first plan (dosar, nomenclator, indicativ, Procese-verbale, Arhivele Naționale), the English UI's own label **Keep in Preluate (days)** (Preluate is the name of a folder the DMS makes inside a watched folder; the channels page says so in one sentence), and the search token `preluare:neconform` that the **Search tips** card prints. The separator sheets and labels the DMS prints carry Romanian words: they may appear only as page thumbnails too small to read, never as an enlarged page. The mailbox picture uses folder names typed in English (`DMS/Taken`, `DMS/Rejected`) instead of the form's Romanian defaults.
8. **Numbers.** The guide prints only limits the screen prints (3 to 500 characters, 1 to 100 copies, 20 MB and 10 pages for a sample). Durations and limits set by the operator (how long files are kept, when a capture is marked old, how many pages a capture may have) are written as "a period your institution sets" or "a limit your institution sets".
9. **Labels of registrations** are printed from the Registry, whose pages do not yet say so: `work/registry-lists.md` gets a section **Print labels** with one picture, and `work/registry.md` names **Print the label**. The separator sheets page links there and does not repeat it.
10. **Home page.** The DMS column keeps five tasks: the handover task is replaced by "Scan the incoming mail and file each letter by rules" linking to the example (selection stays, being yearly work; handover stays in the sidebar). The title has no apostrophe, which the home test's regular expression would meet escaped.
11. **Caps** in `tests/test_dms_content.py`: pages 40 to 50 (39 + 9 = 48), pictures 130 to 165 (123 + 35 = 158).
12. **SQL.** No capture state is set by SQL. The kit's existing `stage()` keeps running before every shot: it marks the reception of newly filed documents as passed, because the demo has no archival converter. It touches the pictures of filed documents (`dms-capture-ex-document`, `dms-capture-doc-mark`, `dms-capture-ex-registration`), which then show what an equipped installation shows; it never changes a capture.

## Global Constraints

- Every Global Constraint of `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-02-dms-user-guide.md` applies: the Keel guide's voice (second person, present tense, numbered steps, one action each); UI labels in bold exactly as `en.ts` writes them; messages in plain quotes; links as absolute site paths with a trailing slash; a lead paragraph of at most 250 characters that says who the page is for and what it covers, before any heading, list or picture; front matter with `title` and `sidebar.label` only and no body H1; full 1600x900 light English pictures referenced as `![caption](../../../assets/screenshots/dms-<id>.png)` from `dms/*.md` and `../../../../assets/...` from `dms/<group>/*.md`, the alt text a full caption without a final period; roles by their UI names; plain copy, no em dashes, no "simply" or "just", no counts the UI does not show.
- Out of date and replaced: "the guide never implies scanning and barcodes". Scanning, barcodes, OCR of zones and handwriting exist now. Still not available, and said once in the FAQ: scanning from a scanner's own program on the computer (TWAIN, WIA), printing labels in a label printer's own language, printing to the DMS printer from a phone, and the DMS sending e-mail.
- The demo note, once on `quickstart-capture.md` and on `examples/incoming-mail.md`: "The pictures were taken in a demo city hall, Example City Hall, with made-up people and documents."
- Features an operator sets up are written as "when your institution has it" or "ask your administrator", never as steps: the watched folder's shared folder and its scanner, a mailbox's network, reading handwriting (a model the operator connects; the guide says only the picture of the field's zone is sent to it), the Keel connection, and the address the virtual printer has.
- No internals (the first plan's list holds) and none of: PaddleOCR, zxing, Ghostscript, RE2, `SDMSSEP`, `SDMSREG`, `.sdms-lucru`, environment variables, IMAP IDLE, OAuth flow details, the audit entry names. Allowed because a person filling the screens needs them: IMAP (the kind of mail server the mailbox form asks for; 993 is its port's default on the form), IPP (the macOS steps on screen name it), Microsoft 365, QR code, Code 128 (a label carries it and a hand scanner types it). A capture's page prints the text each code holds under its page (such as `SDMSSEP:DOC`); pictures may show it, the pages never quote it.
- Roles: **Capture operator** (new), **Registrar** and **Archive administrator** gain capture permissions. Permissions by their UI words: **Process captures**, **Manage capture**.
- Every name, number and date a page prints matches its pictures (registration numbers, part numbers, the run's dates).

## Review Focus

The failure modes no test below catches, and where the plan checks each:

1. **Romanian in a capture picture.** Server sentences, the folder defaults of the mailbox form, and the separator sheets and labels the DMS prints (Romanian words on paper). The kit refuses a shot with a Romanian navigation label or a Romanian diacritic (Task 2 Step 2); Task 4 opens every picture and rejects an enlarged sheet or label.
2. **A label that differs from the UI.** Capture has two sets of stage names: a capture's page uses **Reading**, **Codes**, **Splitting**, **OCR**, **Extraction**, **Validation**, **Routing**, **Filing**, **Keel** (so the button reads **Continue without Codes**), and a flow's steps are **Barcodes**, **Split**, **OCR**, **Extraction**, **Validation**, **Routing**, **Filing**, **Keel**. Tasks 6 and 7 run the label audit per page set and Task 10 Step 1 on the whole guide; every miss is reviewed.
3. **Who sees what.** **To process** lists the captures of channels in units where the person holds both **Process captures** and **Access to documents with personal data**; **Manage capture** shows the channels and their state, never the captures; a person's own captures and printer are theirs alone. Task 10 Step 3 checks every "who sees it" sentence against `nav.ts` and the admin guide's permissions table.
4. **A feature the demo could not run shown as working**, or an operator's number given as a fact: the Keel step starting a process, a mailbox being read, the real handwriting model. Task 10 Steps 2 and 3 read every page for these.
5. **Pictures and text disagree** on names, numbers and dates. Each content task writes its text from the pictures it copies, and Task 4 records the ids and numbers each picture shows in `review-capture.tsv`.
