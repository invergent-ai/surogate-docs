# Quickstart

Once you're signed into Surogate Ops, the welcome screen takes you from a fresh account to your first running agent in under five minutes.

## The welcome screen

First sign-in lands you at the welcome. Click **Start**, then pick how you want to start:

- **Create my first Agent** — drops you on the Work dashboard with a "Find templates" prompt. Pick a template and you have a working agent.
- **Develop and Train Agents** — drops you on the Studio dashboard, where you build agents from scratch.
- **Just exploring** — same destination, no commitment.

You can switch back and forth any time using the **Work / Develop** toggle in the top-right of every Studio page.

## The two surfaces

- **Work** is where you (or your team) actually use agents. Chat, sessions, attached skills, attached knowledge bases. End users live here.
- **Develop** (Studio) is where you build agents and everything that feeds them. Models, skills, knowledge bases, datasets, training runs, monitoring, evaluations. Builders live here.

The toggle remembers your choice for next time.

## Your first agent in five minutes

The fastest path is from a template:

1. Be on the **Work** dashboard.
2. Type what you want in **Describe what you need** — e.g. *an agent that answers customer questions from our help center*.
3. Click **Find Templates**, pick the one closest to your need.
4. The agent is created, configured, and ready to chat. Click into it and send a test message.

For the longer path — defining a model, building a skill, attaching a knowledge base by hand — see [Use cases](../use-cases/customer-support.md) for end-to-end walkthroughs.
