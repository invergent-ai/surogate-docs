Profile

The tab opens with "Your account details, avatar, and connected tools." — the account fields sit in an Account details card with the action row (Save changes / Reset / Change Password) pinned in its footer.

FieldNotes
Full nameDisplay name. Editable.
UsernameLocked. Cannot be changed.
EmailLogin. Changing requires re-verification.
RoleFree-form. Shows next to your avatar in the platform header.
BioShort freeform, capped at 180 characters.
CompanyOptional.
AvatarUpload a new photo, choose a preset avatar, or remove the current one.
Change PasswordSends a reset link by email. After setting a new password you're signed out and need to sign in again.

The USERNAME field is locked and shows "Cannot be changed." next to it. Everything else (full name, email, role, bio, company) is editable inline; click Save changes to persist.
