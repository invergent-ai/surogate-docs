---
title: Groups
---

A group is a set of people you give the same access, such as "Editors" or "Help desk". You need the **Administrator** role.

**Groups** lists your groups with how many members each has.

## Create a group

1. Press **New group**.
2. Type its **Name** and press **Create group**.

The console opens the new group. Names are unique: a second group with the same name says **A group with this name already exists.**

## Members

On a group's page, the **Members** card lists who is in it.

1. Type a name or an email in **Search for users to add**. Matching users appear under the field.
2. Press the **+** beside each person to add them. Press the **×** beside a member to remove them.
3. Press **Save members**. Nothing changes until you save.

## Rename or delete a group

- Press **Rename**, type the new **Name** and press **Save**.
- Press **Delete group**, then **Delete group** again to confirm. The members stay; only the group goes.
