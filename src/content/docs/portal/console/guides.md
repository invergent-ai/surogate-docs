---
title: Guides
description: The user guides the portal shows, where each applies and how it starts.
---

**Guides** lists the portal's [user guides](/portal/sites/guides/) for administrators: each guide's name, how it starts (when asked for, or on the first visit), its number of steps, and the sites and pages it applies to. To see one, open one of its pages with `?guide=<id>` at the end of its address.

**New guide** makes a guide and **Edit** on a guide changes it: where it shows, how it starts and its steps, each pointing at a block of its pages. See [Write a guide](/portal/sites/guides/#write-a-guide). A portal has at most 50 guides; at 50, **New guide** is off.

A guide that is saved but not running, because of a problem, is listed apart, under **Guides not running**, with **Edit** to open it and see why.
