---
title: Guides
description: The user guides the portal shows, where each applies and how it starts.
---

**Guides** lists the portal's [user guides](/portal/sites/guides/) for administrators: each guide's name, how it starts (when asked for, or on the first visit), its number of steps, and the sites and pages it applies to. Guides are written in the portal's files; to see one, open one of its pages with `?guide=<id>`.
