## Before you start

- The **Sales CRM** tables (or the Sales desk), with your target accounts in **Companies**.
- A partners owner named on How we work, and any partner terms written there.
- The [rules](/superstar/how-it-works/#the-rules) every agent keeps.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Keep a Partners table**

   Add a page **Partners** with a table: Partner, Track (Referral, Co-sell, Reseller), Stage, Contact, Owner. Name the partners owner on **How we work**.

2. **Applications**

   Give a partner's application to **@Inbound qualifier**: it researches them, recommends Referral, Co-sell or Reseller, adds them to **Partners** and drafts the invite to a screening call.

3. **Paths**

   Ask **@Relationships** which partner knows someone at your target accounts. Each path goes to **Paths in**.

4. **Ask**

   The request goes to the partner, never to their client, with a note they can forward. A deal they bring gets Source **Partner** on **Deals**.

</Steps>

## The template

**Sales CRM** is a section. Open it from **Templates** in the sidebar. It makes **Deals** (with a **Source** that includes Partner), **Companies**, **People**, **Signals**, **Paths in** (a path's connector can be a partner), **Inbound leads** and **Do not contact**. The **Partners** page is yours to add: the agents find it by that name.

## Prompts

Copy one and change the names. A prompt that starts with an @name goes in a block on the page; the others go in **Ask** (**Ctrl J**).

```text title="A block that starts with @Inbound qualifier"
@Inbound qualifier Brightline Analytics applied to partner with us. Research them, recommend a track and draft the screening invite.
```

```text title="A block that starts with @Relationships"
@Relationships Which of our partners know someone at our Tier 1 accounts? Map them in Paths in and draft the ask for the strongest one.
```

## What good looks like

"You know the risk data team at Castell Bank well. Would you introduce us to Elena Varga? Here is a note you can forward as it is." One account, one person, an easy no.

An application handled well: Brightline Analytics in **Partners** at Stage **Applied**, Track **Referral**, with one paragraph on why, three questions for the screening call on its row's page, and the invite drafted from the partners owner.

## Watch for

- Never quote commission or terms that aren't on How we work.
- Write to the partner, never around them to their client.

## What to count

- Deals sourced by partners
- Win rate of partner deals against outbound

Filter **Deals** by **Source**: Partner against Outbound, and compare how many reach **Won**.

<Aside type="note" title="What the numbers say">
In the Ebsta and Pavilion 2025 benchmark (655,000 opportunities), partner referrals were the most efficient channel: win rate times deal size over cycle length came to 1.3 times the average, against 1.05 for outbound ([Ebsta](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf)).
</Aside>
