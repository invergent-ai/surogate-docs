## Before you start

- The company as a row in **Companies**. Its row opens as its own page, where the brief goes.
- A deal room for the deal, if there is one already.
- The [rules](/superstar/how-it-works/#the-rules) every agent keeps.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Ask on the company page**

   Open the company's page from its row in **Companies** and ask **@Signal scout** for a brief. It writes it as blocks under your ask: who they are, what they're likely trying to do, what fits, three questions, the risks, the sources.

2. **What you already have**

   It searches Superstar first for deals, people and customers you already have with them or their group. A link through their group is often the best way in.

3. **Find the others**

   Ask who else is in the buying group, and add them to the deal room's **People**. New deals average eight people.

4. **Before the call**

   Open the sources you'll quote. Every number says the period it covers.

</Steps>

## The template

**Sales CRM** is a section. Open it from **Templates** in the sidebar. It makes **Deals**, **Companies**, **People**, **Signals**, **Paths in**, **Inbound leads** and **Do not contact**. Each Companies row opens as a page: the brief and the Signal scout's dated findings stay there as the account's running record.

## Prompts

Copy one and change the names. A prompt that starts with an @name goes in a block on the page; the others go in **Ask** (**Ctrl J**).

```text title="A block that starts with @Signal scout"
@Signal scout Brief me for Thursday's first call with Marisol Energy: who they are, what they're trying to do this year, what fits, three questions, risks, sources.
```

```text title="A block that starts with @Signal scout"
@Signal scout Who else at Marisol Energy is likely in the buying group for meter data quality? Names, roles and sources.
```

## What good looks like

Three questions you couldn't have asked without the brief, each tied to something they published, with its link.

For Marisol Energy, questions like these:

> 1. Your Q3 report names meter data quality as a rollout risk. Which part worries you most: missing reads or wrong ones?
> 2. You're replacing 1.2 million meters by 2028. Who owns the data from the old ones once they're off?
> 3. Your regulator's new reporting deadline is in March. What would have to be true by then?

Each one starts from their own words, so the call starts from their problem.

## Watch for

- The brief is the agent's reading of public pages: open a source before you quote it.
- Bring the questions, not the brief: the call is about them.

## What to count

- First meetings with a brief
- People per deal after the first meeting

Count the People rows on each deal room after the first call. The Ebsta and Pavilion 2025 benchmark found eight people on an average new deal, and that 44% of the contacts sellers deal with never reach the CRM ([Ebsta](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf)).
