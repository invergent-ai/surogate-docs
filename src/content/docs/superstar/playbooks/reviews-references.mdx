## Before you start

- Customers with a pilot or a contract that runs well, as **Won** rows in **Deals**.
- The account owner's mailbox connected.
- The [rules](/superstar/how-it-works/#the-rules) every agent keeps.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Pick the customers**

   From **Won** deals with a pilot that runs well. Ask the account owner who is happy.

2. **Ask**

   **@Follow-up keeper** drafts a short ask from the account owner: a review on the site your buyers read, or one reference call a quarter. Read it, fix it, send it from your mailbox.

3. **Keep it**

   What they agreed to goes in **Case studies** and **Logo permissions** (the **Customer proof** section), with who said yes and when.

4. **Use it**

   Ask **@Content producer** for a post or a one-line quote for deal rooms from an approved review.

</Steps>

## The template

**Customer proof** is a section. Open it from **Templates** in the sidebar. It makes **Case studies** (Customer, Result, Quote, Approved, Link) and **Logo permissions** (Company, May show, Who said yes, When).
## Prompts

Copy one and change the names. A prompt that starts with an @name goes in a block on the page; the others go in **Ask** (**Ctrl J**).

```text title="A block that starts with @Follow-up keeper"
@Follow-up keeper Draft a short ask from Maya to Kestrel Insurance's claims lead: one reference call a quarter with insurers we're talking to, and an easy no.
```

```text title="A block that starts with @Content producer"
@Content producer Turn Kestrel's approved quote into a LinkedIn post and a one-line quote for deal rooms.
```

## What good looks like

"Would you take one 20-minute call a quarter with an insurer who's where you were a year ago? I'll brief you first, and no is fine." A small, clear ask.

A reference kept on record: Kestrel Insurance in **Case studies** with the quote they approved and **Approved** ticked, and in **Logo permissions** with **May show** ticked, who said yes and the date.

## Watch for

- Never offer anything in return for a review. Check the review site's rules first.
- Don't send the same reference twice in a month.

## What to count

- References available per segment
- Deals that used one

Count approved rows in **Case studies** by industry, and note on each deal's page when a reference call happened.

<Aside type="note" title="What the numbers say">
In TrustRadius's 2026 survey of 1,862 B2B buyers, 74% used reviews and 53% spoke with a peer before buying, and vendor material ranked last among sources they trust ([HG Insights](https://hginsights.com/blog/trust-more-verify-everything-what-the-2026-b2b-buying-disconnect-report-says-about-where-buying-is-actually-heading/)).
</Aside>
