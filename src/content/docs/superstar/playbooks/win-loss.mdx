## Before you start

- A **Deals** row and a deal room page (Templates → **Deal room**) for each deal you close.
- A **Positioning and messaging** page for the changes to land on.
- The [rules](/superstar/how-it-works/#the-rules) every agent keeps.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Within a month**

   When a deal turns **Won** or **Lost**, write the **Lost reason** on its row and ask **@Follow-up keeper** to draft a short request from the owner for a 20-minute call.

2. **Notes on the deal room**

   Paste what they said under **Call notes** on the deal room page, in their words.

3. **Monthly**

   Ask **@Strategist** to read the month's notes: the reasons, the competitors named, and what to change in **Positioning and messaging**.

</Steps>

## The template

**Deal room** is a page. Open it from **Templates** in the sidebar. It makes a **People** table and headings for **Call notes** and the **Proposal**. The win or loss call goes under Call notes, like any other call.

## Prompts

Copy one and change the names. A prompt that starts with an @name goes in a block on the page; the others go in **Ask** (**Ctrl J**).

```text title="A block that starts with @Follow-up keeper"
@Follow-up keeper We lost Ostra Retail. Draft a short note from the deal owner asking for 20 minutes on why, with two times next week.
```

```text title="A block that starts with @Strategist"
@Strategist Read this month's win and loss notes on the deal rooms: the reasons, the competitors named, and three changes to Positioning and messaging.
```

## What good looks like

"Lost Ostra Retail on price against a cheaper tool, but their buyer called our pilot report the clearest they saw. Change: lead with the report, offer a smaller first pilot." A reason in the buyer's words, and a change.

One line per deal, then the change. A month with five closed deals gives five lines and one or two changes, each on the page it changes.

## Watch for

- Ask lost deals too; they say more.
- A win or loss call is for listening, not a second pitch.

## What to count

- Closed deals with a win or loss call
- Changes made from them

Filter **Deals** by Stage **Won** or **Lost** for the month, and count the deal rooms with notes from the call.

<Aside type="note" title="What the numbers say">
In Klue's 2025 survey, 70% of win/loss programs analyse a deal within a month of closing it ([Klue](https://klue.com/blog/win-loss-analysis-statistics)). In Crayon's 2024 survey, 55% rated win/loss findings extremely valuable for competitive work ([Crayon](https://crayon.co/hubfs/Algert/2024%20State%20of%20Competitive%20Intelligence/Competitive%20Intelligence%20Report%202024_PDF.pdf)). Both are vendors: read them as direction.
</Aside>
