## Before you start

- The **Sales CRM** tables (or the Sales desk), with your **Won** and **Lost** deals in **Deals** and their people in **People**.
- Owners on How we work, so each move reaches someone.
- The [rules](/superstar/how-it-works/#the-rules) every agent keeps.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Name them**

   On **People**, keep everyone from **Won** deals and from good **Lost** ones, with the company they were at.

2. **Ask monthly**

   Ask **@Signal scout** where each one works now, with a public source. A move goes to **Signals** as **Leadership**, **Act now**, routed to the owner. Press **↻** to repeat the ask every month.

3. **Write as yourself**

   Ask **@Relationships** for a note from the person who knew them: congratulations, one line on what changed, no pitch. Read it, fix it, send it from your mailbox.

</Steps>

## The template

**Sales CRM** is a section. Open it from **Templates** in the sidebar. It makes seven pages, each one table: **Deals**, **Companies**, **People**, **Signals**, **Paths in**, **Inbound leads** and **Do not contact**. A move is a Signals row with Type **Leadership**; the new company goes to Companies.

## Prompts

Copy one and change the names. A prompt that starts with an @name goes in a block on the page; the others go in **Ask** (**Ctrl J**).

```text title="A block that starts with @Signal scout"
@Signal scout Check where the people from our Won deals in the last two years work now. Public sources only; log each move in Signals.
```

```text title="A block that starts with @Relationships"
@Relationships Draft a note from Tom to Ingrid Holm, who ran our pilot at Nordlys Shipping and just joined Marisol Energy as head of data. Congratulations, one line on what we did together, no pitch.
```

## What good looks like

"Congratulations on Marisol Energy, Ingrid. The duplicate checks we set up at Nordlys still run every night. Happy to compare notes once you've settled in." Personal, true, and easy to answer.

A move logged well in **Signals**:

| Found | Account | Type | What happened | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-18 | Marisol Energy | Leadership | Ingrid Holm, who ran the Nordlys Shipping pilot, joined as head of data (link) | Act now |

The note goes from Tom, who worked with her, not from whoever owns Marisol Energy.

## Watch for

- Public sources only. If you can't link where they work now, it isn't a signal.
- One note from the person who knew them, not a sequence.

## What to count

- Moves found
- Meetings from moves

Count Signals rows of Type **Leadership** that name a former customer, and the Deals that came from them.

<Aside type="note" title="What the numbers say">
UserGems reports that deals with a former champion are 114% more likely to be won and 54% larger ([UserGems](https://www.usergems.com/blog/how-much-are-previous-champions-worth)). That is a vendor measuring its own customers, with no control group: read it as direction, not a promise.
</Aside>
