The business app, **Keel**, at https://keel-app.surogate.ai, is where a project's staff work cases. You sign in with your work account of the project. Your supervisor gives you the link to your project, or a link to set your password.

## Your first sign-in: the set-password link

When a supervisor or a project owner invites you, they send you a **set-password link**.

1. Open the link. The page is titled **Set the password for** your email: "Choose the password you will sign in with."
2. Type a **Password** and type it again in **Repeat the password**.
3. Press **Set password**.

The app signs you in and opens your **Work** page. The link works once, until the time your supervisor was shown. A used or expired link says **This link cannot be used**: "This link is not valid or has expired. Ask your supervisor for a new one."

If you open the link while signed in, the page says **You are signed in**: "To set a password from this link, sign out first, then open the link again." It offers **Open your work** and **Sign out**.

## Signing in

The sign-in page is titled **Sign in to** and your project's name: "Use your work account."

1. Type your **Email** and your **Password**.
2. Leave **Remember me** on to stay signed in on this browser.
3. Press **Sign in**.

When your project signs in with Google or GitHub, the page also shows **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub**. Some projects show only those buttons.

If sign-in does not work, the page says why:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Wrong email or password.** | Check both and try again. |
| **Too many attempts. Wait a minute and try again.** | Wait, then try again. |
| **You have no account in** *project***. Ask a supervisor to invite you.** | Your Google or GitHub account is not one of the project's. Ask to be invited. |
| **This project does not exist.** | Open the link your supervisor sent you. |
| **This project has no way to sign in yet. Ask a supervisor.** | The project's sign-in is not set up. |
| **Cannot reach Surogate. Try again.** | A temporary problem; try again. |

If the app cannot tell which project you mean, it asks **Which project?**: "Open the link your supervisor sent you."

## Your projects

One sign-in serves every project you are on the staff of. The switcher at the top of the left rail lists them by name; pick one to open the same page in that project, with no new sign-in. The app opens the project you used last.

If the app does not let you in, it says why:

- **You are not on any project's staff yet**: "Ask a supervisor to add you."
- **You are not on this project's staff**: "Ask a supervisor to add you."
- **This project does not exist, or it is not yours**: the link is wrong, or you were removed.

## The left rail

| Row | Who sees it | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| **Work** | Everyone | Your items, your team queues and the agents at work. Its badge counts what needs you. See [The Work page](/keel/business/work/). |
| **Cases** | Everyone | Every case of the project, and new cases. See [Cases](/keel/business/cases/). |
| **Team** | Supervisors | Staff, queues, agents and authority limits. See [The Team page](/keel/business/supervisors/). |

The bar at the bottom shows your name and the sign-out button. Supervisors also have **Open Keel Builder**, which opens the project in Keel Builder; using it needs a Surogate account that is a member of the project.

## Phones

The business app works in a phone browser. On a phone, the rail opens from the top bar, and lists and details show one at a time.
