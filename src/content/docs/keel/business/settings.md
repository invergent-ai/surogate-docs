**Your settings** is where you choose which emails Keel sends you about your work. It says: "Keel tells you about your work here, in Work, and by email. Choose which emails you get: Needs me and Updates always show everything, and emails go out as things happen, at any hour."

Open it with **Notification settings** in the header of the [Work page](/keel/business/work/), or with **Your settings** in the bar at the bottom of the left rail. The choices are yours alone, and for the project you have open: "These choices are for this project only." Someone on the staff of two projects chooses for each.

## What a notice is

A **notice** is Keel telling you something about your work: that an item reached you, that a deadline is near, that a colleague mentioned you. Every notice shows in Keel, in [Needs me](/keel/business/work/#notices-in-needs-me) or in [Updates](/keel/business/work/#updates). A notice can also reach you by email. The page chooses which ones do.

## The emails

The table **Emails** has one switch for each kind of notice. Every switch is on until you turn it off, and a change is saved at once: Keel says **Turned off:** or **Turned on:** and the notice.

| Notice | When Keel emails you |
|---|---|
| **An item reaches me** | A step, a vote or an agent's question is given to you. |
| **An item reaches one of my queues** | One email for each queue every few minutes, listing what arrived. |
| **An agent asks one of my queues** | One email for each queue every few minutes, listing the questions. |
| **A goal nears** | An item you hold, or one waiting in your queue, nears its goal. |
| **A deadline nears** | An item you hold, or one waiting in your queue, nears its deadline. |
| **A case I follow moves** | Changes close together make one email. |
| **Someone mentions me** | A colleague mentions you in a comment in a case's document. |

Turning a switch off stops the email, never the notice: **Needs me** and **Updates** always show everything. There are no quiet hours; emails go out as things happen, at any hour.

If Keel has been set up to send no email, the page says: "This installation sends no email yet. Your choices are kept, and apply once it does."

## Emails while you are away

While you are [away](/keel/business/work/#delegate-while-away), the emails about the queues you work go to your substitute, and to no one when you have none. An item that stays with you is mailed to no one. A colleague's mention of you, and a case you follow that moved, still come to you.
